Laws: Formally codified norms enforced by the state through official sanctions

Values: Deeply held beliefs about what is good, right, and desirable in society
Culture: The shared beliefs, values, norms, symbols, language, and practices of a group or society
Material Culture: Physical objects, artifacts, and technology created and used by a society
Non-Material Culture: Intangible aspects of culture including beliefs, values, norms, and language

Ethnocentrism: Judging other cultures by the standards of one's own culture

Counterculture: A subculture that actively opposes dominant cultural values and norms

Ideology: A system of ideas and ideals that form the basis of economic or political thinking
