Cell Biology: Cell biology is the study of cells, their structure, function, and interactions with their environment. It encompasses various sub-disciplines such as cell physiology, cell signaling, and cell cycle regulation.
Genetics: Genetics is the study of genes, heredity, and genetic variation in living organisms. It explores how traits are passed from one generation to the next and the role of genes in determining phenotype.
Ecology: Ecology is the study of interactions between organisms and their environment. It examines relationships such as predator-prey interactions, competition, and symbiosis, as well as broader concepts like ecosystem dynamics and conservation biology.
Evolutionary Biology: Evolutionary biology is the study of the processes that lead to the diversity of life on Earth. It explores mechanisms such as natural selection, genetic drift, and speciation, as well as the patterns of evolution observed in the fossil record.
Physiology: Physiology is the study of the functions and mechanisms of living organisms and their parts. It covers topics such as organ systems, homeostasis, and physiological responses to environmental stimuli.
Anatomy: Anatomy is the study of the structure and organization of living organisms. It includes gross anatomy, which examines structures visible to the naked eye, and microscopic anatomy, which explores tissues and cells.
Biochemistry: Biochemistry is the study of the chemical processes and substances that occur within living organisms. It investigates topics such as metabolism, enzyme kinetics, and the structure-function relationships of biomolecules.
Microbiology: Microbiology is the study of microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, fungi, and protists. It encompasses various sub-disciplines such as medical microbiology, environmental microbiology, and microbial genetics.
Immunology: Immunology is the study of the immune system and its function in protecting the body from pathogens and foreign substances. It explores topics such as immune response mechanisms, vaccination, and autoimmune diseases.
Biotechnology: Biotechnology is the application of biological knowledge and techniques to develop products and processes for various fields such as medicine, agriculture, and industry. It includes genetic engineering, bioprocessing, and bioinformatics
